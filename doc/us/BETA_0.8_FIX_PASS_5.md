A deliberately fabricated but internally consistent dataset still depends on the trusted job executing the measurement process; it is not an independent physical measurement certificate. Do not compare ADC, ADCS and authenticated HTTPS as equal workload units.
- **Hardening:** `TSAN` and `Clang/libFuzzer` remain required GitHub-hosted Ubuntu 24.04 jobs; C++ Debug/Release, ASan/UBSan, WebAdmin, CodeQL and disposable VM remain required on their declared Ubuntu 22.04/24.04/26.04 images. Unsupported sanitizer runtimes, unavailable toolchains, skipped cases or crashes are **blockers**. Local synthetic wrapper tests cannot certify a sanitizer execution.
- **Resource/drain:** Existing slow reader, half-open TLS, SIGTERM/restart, bounded queue and protected healthy-peer tests must complete at one final SHA. The 1 MiB fuzz log, 512 MiB fuzzer RSS limit and 180-second wall cap do **not** establish full denial-of-service resistance.

High/critical unreviewed advisories block acceptance.
